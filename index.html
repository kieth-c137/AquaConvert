🪸 About AquaConvert

AquaConvert turns one unit into another without leaving the reef. It handles length, weight, temperature, volume, and time, and works entirely in your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere.

Type any whole or decimal number and the result updates as you type. Every unit is converted through a common base unit (meters, kilograms, liters, seconds), and temperature uses its own formulas, so results stay accurate.
